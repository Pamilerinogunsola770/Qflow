Read it from the center

QFlow has one source of truth.

  • main.jsxMounts the app inside QFlowProvider.
  • QFlowContextOwns visits, queues, lifecycle rules, routing, metrics, and live sync.
  • interfaces/Each view reads the same state and dispatches operational actions.
  • theme/Stores the semantic palette and queue/status constants.
  • App.jsxActs as the prototype shell and switches between client views.

Data flow

Every interface speaks to the context. No view owns a duplicate queue or invents its own patient status.

Four interfaces, four jobs

The system stays focused on movement logistics. Clinical records and treatment data never enter this state model.

Patient

Minimal, mobile-first guidance for one person's visit.

  • Ticket and status
  • Current location
  • People ahead and wait
  • Called instructions

Staff

Dense queue control for one service point.

  • Priority-ordered queue
  • Call and start service
  • Complete or route
  • Occupancy awareness

Reception

Fast entry and visit lookup at the front desk.

  • Manual check-in
  • Unique ticket
  • Active visit search
  • Current stage

Overview

Facility-wide visibility for supervisors.

  • Active and completed totals
  • Queue lengths
  • Wait estimates
  • Congestion signals

What the context stores

These are logistics records: where a visit is, how it moves, and how long the queue is likely to take.

servicePoints

Configured locations with availability, average service time, active visit, and activeQueue entry IDs.

visits

Visit identity, ticket, priority, current service point, current status, check-in time, completion time, and visited locations.

queueEntries

Historical movement records with joined, called, service-started, and service-ended timestamps.

priorityRank

Emergency floats before Urgent, which floats before Normal. Equal priorities remain FIFO.

getQueuePosition

Finds a visit's live position in the current service-point queue.

getWaitMinutes

Returns position multiplied by that service point's average service time.
